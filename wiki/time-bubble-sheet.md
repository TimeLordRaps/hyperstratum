# The time-bubble sheet

**Status, 2026-10-06.** A pointer page. The proposal is the owner's (Tyler Roost, USER-STATED across 2026-10-03 to 2026-10-06): a bubble whose boundary is a sheet, with a finite space inside that is frozen or unbounded in time, entangled with the outside, hosting a computation that doubles as its own corrective demon. The analysis lives in the owning repositories and the passages below are live references. Tags are the family's: `[FORM]` checked, `[FRAME]` holds in a stated model, `[HYPER]` proposal, `[OPEN]` unresolved.
